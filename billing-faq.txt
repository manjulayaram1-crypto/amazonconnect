BILLING AND PAYMENT FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS - ABC INSURANCE

Question: When is my payment due?
Answer: Your payment is due on the same date each month, shown on your policy documents and billing statements. You can also check your due date in your online account or mobile app.

Question: What payment methods do you accept?
Answer: We accept credit cards including Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover. We also accept debit cards, bank transfers, electronic checks, and paper checks.

Question: How do I set up automatic payments?
Answer: To set up autopay, log into your online account, go to Billing, and select Set Up Autopay. You can use a credit card or bank account. You'll receive a confirmation email once it's set up.

Question: How do I change my payment method?
Answer: To change your payment method, log into your online account and go to Billing then Payment Methods. You can add a new payment method and set it as your default.

Question: What happens if I miss a payment?
Answer: If you miss a payment, you'll receive a reminder notice. You typically have a 10 day grace period. After that, a late fee may apply and your coverage could be at risk of cancellation.

Question: Is there a late fee?
Answer: Yes, a late fee of $15 may be charged if your payment is not received within the grace period. To avoid late fees, consider setting up automatic payments.

Question: How do I get a copy of my bill?
Answer: You can view and download your bills from your online account, request them through our mobile app, or call customer service to have a copy mailed or emailed to you.

Question: Can I change my due date?
Answer: In some cases, we can adjust your payment due date. Please call customer service at 1-800-555-0123 to request a due date change. Changes may take one billing cycle to take effect.

Question: Do you offer payment plans?
Answer: Yes, we offer monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, and annual payment options. Paying annually or semi-annually may save you money on installment fees.

Question: Why did my premium change?
Answer: Premiums can change for several reasons including rate adjustments, changes to your policy, moving to a new address, adding or removing vehicles, or changes to your claims history.

Question: How do I get a refund?
Answer: If you're owed a refund, it will be sent automatically within 7 to 10 business days. Refunds are typically sent to the same payment method used for your last payment or by check.

Question: Is there a fee for paying by credit card?
Answer: No, we do not charge a fee for credit card payments. However, your credit card company may have their own policies regarding fees.

Question: How do I view my payment history?
Answer: You can view your payment history by logging into your online account and going to Billing then Payment History. You can also request a payment history by calling customer service.

Question: Can I pay my bill by phone?
Answer: Yes, you can pay by phone by calling 1-800-555-0123 and following the automated prompts, or by speaking with a customer service representative.

Question: What is an installment fee?
Answer: An installment fee is a small charge added when you pay monthly instead of paying your premium in full. Paying annually eliminates this fee and may save you money.
