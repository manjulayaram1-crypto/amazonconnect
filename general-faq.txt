GENERAL FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS - ABC INSURANCE

Question: What are your business hours?
Answer: Our customer service is available Monday through Friday, 8 AM to 6 PM Central Time. For emergencies, we have 24/7 claims support available at 1-800-555-CLAIM.

Question: How do I contact customer service?
Answer: You can reach us by phone at 1-800-555-0123, by email at support@abcinsurance.com, through our website chat, or by visiting one of our local offices.

Question: How do I update my contact information?
Answer: You can update your contact information by logging into your account on our website, calling customer service at 1-800-555-0123, or speaking with an agent.

Question: How do I reset my online account password?
Answer: To reset your password, go to our website login page and click Forgot Password. Enter your email address and we'll send you a reset link. The link expires in 24 hours.

Question: What is my policy number?
Answer: Your policy number is located on your insurance card, on any billing statement, or in your online account under Policy Details. It typically starts with POL followed by numbers.

Question: How do I get proof of insurance?
Answer: You can get proof of insurance by downloading your insurance card from our mobile app, printing it from your online account, or requesting it by calling customer service.

Question: Do you have a mobile app?
Answer: Yes, we have a free mobile app available for both iPhone and Android. You can download it from the App Store or Google Play. Search for ABC Insurance.

Question: How do I cancel my policy?
Answer: To cancel your policy, please call customer service at 1-800-555-0123. We'll need to verify your identity and discuss any refund you may be entitled to.

Question: Can I add someone to my policy?
Answer: Yes, you can add drivers to your auto policy or additional insureds to other policies. Call customer service or log into your online account to make changes.

Question: How do I get a copy of my policy documents?
Answer: You can view and download your policy documents from your online account, request them through our mobile app, or call customer service to have copies mailed to you.
