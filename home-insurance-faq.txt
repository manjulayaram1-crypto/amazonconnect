HOME INSURANCE FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS - ABC INSURANCE

Question: What does home insurance cover?
Answer: Home insurance typically covers your dwelling, other structures like garages, personal belongings, liability protection, and additional living expenses if your home becomes uninhabitable.

Question: What is dwelling coverage?
Answer: Dwelling coverage pays to repair or rebuild your home if it's damaged by covered events like fire, windstorm, hail, or vandalism. It covers the structure itself including walls, roof, and built-in appliances.

Question: What is personal property coverage?
Answer: Personal property coverage protects your belongings like furniture, clothing, electronics, and appliances. It covers them whether they're damaged at home or anywhere in the world.

Question: What is liability coverage for home insurance?
Answer: Liability coverage protects you if someone is injured on your property or if you accidentally damage someone else's property. It covers legal fees and medical expenses up to your policy limit.

Question: What is not covered by home insurance?
Answer: Standard home insurance typically does not cover floods, earthquakes, normal wear and tear, pest damage, or intentional damage. Separate policies are available for flood and earthquake coverage.

Question: Do I need flood insurance?
Answer: Standard home insurance does not cover flood damage. If you live in a flood-prone area, we strongly recommend purchasing separate flood insurance through the National Flood Insurance Program or a private insurer.

Question: What is replacement cost vs actual cash value?
Answer: Replacement cost pays to replace your items with new ones of similar kind and quality. Actual cash value pays the current value of items minus depreciation. Replacement cost provides better protection.

Question: How much home insurance do I need?
Answer: You should have enough dwelling coverage to rebuild your home completely. We recommend coverage equal to 100% of your home's replacement cost, not its market value.

Question: What is an insurance deductible for home insurance?
Answer: Your deductible is the amount you pay out of pocket before insurance kicks in. Common deductibles are $500, $1000, or $2500. Higher deductibles mean lower premiums.

Question: Does home insurance cover my home business?
Answer: Standard home insurance provides limited coverage for business equipment at home. If you run a home business, you may need additional coverage. Contact us to discuss your needs.

Question: Am I covered if a tree falls on my house?
Answer: Yes, if a tree falls on your house due to wind or other covered events, your dwelling coverage will pay for repairs minus your deductible. We also cover removal of the tree from your home.

Question: Does home insurance cover dog bites?
Answer: Yes, liability coverage typically covers dog bite injuries to others. However, some breeds may be excluded or require additional coverage. Check your policy or call us for details.

Question: What is loss of use coverage?
Answer: Loss of use coverage, also called additional living expenses, pays for temporary housing, meals, and other costs if you cannot live in your home while it's being repaired after a covered loss.

Question: How do I file a home insurance claim?
Answer: To file a home insurance claim, call 1-800-555-CLAIM, use our mobile app, or file online. Take photos of damage, make temporary repairs to prevent further damage, and keep receipts.

Question: Does home insurance cover my detached garage?
Answer: Yes, other structures coverage in your home policy covers detached garages, sheds, fences, and other structures on your property. It's typically 10% of your dwelling coverage.
