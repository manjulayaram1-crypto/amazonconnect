LIFE INSURANCE FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS - ABC INSURANCE

Question: What is life insurance?
Answer: Life insurance is a contract where you pay premiums and in return, the insurance company pays a death benefit to your beneficiaries when you pass away. It provides financial protection for your loved ones.

Question: What is term life insurance?
Answer: Term life insurance provides coverage for a specific period, such as 10, 20, or 30 years. If you pass away during the term, your beneficiaries receive the death benefit. It's typically the most affordable option.

Question: What is whole life insurance?
Answer: Whole life insurance provides coverage for your entire life and includes a cash value component that grows over time. Premiums are higher than term life but remain level for life.

Question: What is a beneficiary?
Answer: A beneficiary is the person or people you designate to receive the death benefit when you pass away. You can name multiple beneficiaries and specify how the benefit is divided.

Question: How much life insurance do I need?
Answer: A common guideline is 10 to 12 times your annual income. Consider your debts, mortgage, children's education costs, and how many years your family would need financial support.

Question: What is a death benefit?
Answer: The death benefit is the amount of money your beneficiaries receive when you pass away. It's typically paid as a lump sum and is generally not subject to income tax.

Question: Do I need a medical exam for life insurance?
Answer: Many policies require a medical exam, but we also offer no-exam policies with simplified underwriting. No-exam policies may have higher premiums or lower coverage limits.

Question: Can I change my beneficiary?
Answer: Yes, you can change your beneficiary at any time by filling out a beneficiary change form. Log into your online account or call customer service to request the form.

Question: What happens if I stop paying premiums?
Answer: If you stop paying premiums on term life insurance, your coverage will lapse after a grace period, typically 30 days. Whole life policies may have options to use cash value to pay premiums.

Question: Can I borrow against my life insurance?
Answer: If you have whole life insurance with accumulated cash value, you can borrow against it. Loans accrue interest and unpaid loans reduce your death benefit.

Question: What is cash value in life insurance?
Answer: Cash value is a savings component in permanent life insurance policies that grows over time. You can borrow against it or surrender the policy for its cash value.

Question: Is the death benefit taxable?
Answer: Life insurance death benefits are generally not subject to federal income tax. However, if the benefit is paid to your estate rather than a named beneficiary, it may be subject to estate taxes.

Question: Can I have multiple life insurance policies?
Answer: Yes, you can have multiple life insurance policies from different companies. Many people layer policies to meet different needs at different life stages.

Question: What is the grace period for premium payments?
Answer: Most life insurance policies have a 30 day grace period for premium payments. Your coverage remains active during this time, but you should pay as soon as possible to avoid lapse.

Question: How do I file a life insurance claim?
Answer: To file a life insurance claim, call 1-800-555-0123. You'll need the policy number, a certified copy of the death certificate, and the claimant's identification. Claims are typically paid within 30 days.
