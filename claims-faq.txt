CLAIMS FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS - ABC INSURANCE

Question: How do I file a claim?
Answer: You can file a claim by calling our claims hotline at 1-800-555-CLAIM, through our mobile app, on our website, or by speaking with an agent. Have your policy number and incident details ready.

Question: How long does a claim take to process?
Answer: Most claims are processed within 7 to 14 business days. Complex claims may take longer. You'll receive updates via email and can check status anytime online or by phone.

Question: What information do I need to file a claim?
Answer: To file a claim, you'll need your policy number, date and location of incident, description of what happened, photos if available, police report number for accidents or theft, and contact information for any other parties involved.

Question: How do I check my claim status?
Answer: You can check your claim status by calling our claims line at 1-800-555-CLAIM, logging into your online account, using our mobile app, or asking our automated phone system with your claim number.

Question: What happens after I file a claim?
Answer: After filing, a claims adjuster will be assigned to your case. They will contact you within 24 to 48 hours to discuss the claim, may schedule an inspection, and will guide you through the process.

Question: What is a claims adjuster?
Answer: A claims adjuster is a professional who investigates your claim, assesses the damage, and determines how much the insurance company will pay. They work to resolve your claim fairly and quickly.

Question: How long do I have to file a claim?
Answer: You should file a claim as soon as possible after an incident, ideally within 24 to 48 hours. Most policies require claims to be filed within a reasonable time, typically 30 to 60 days.

Question: Can I choose my own repair shop?
Answer: Yes, you can choose your own repair shop for auto repairs. However, using one of our preferred network shops may offer benefits like guaranteed repairs and faster processing.

Question: What if I disagree with the claim decision?
Answer: If you disagree with a claim decision, you can request a review by contacting your claims adjuster or their supervisor. You can also file an appeal in writing explaining why you disagree.

Question: Will filing a claim affect my premium?
Answer: Filing a claim may affect your premium depending on the type of claim, who was at fault, and your claims history. Not-at-fault claims and comprehensive claims typically have less impact.

Question: What is a total loss?
Answer: A total loss occurs when the cost to repair your vehicle exceeds its actual cash value. In this case, we pay you the vehicle's value minus your deductible instead of repairing it.

Question: How do I get my claim payment?
Answer: Claim payments can be sent by check, direct deposit, or paid directly to repair shops or medical providers. You can specify your preference when filing the claim.

Question: What if the other driver was at fault?
Answer: If another driver was at fault, you can file a claim with their insurance company or with us. We can help you through the process and may seek reimbursement from the at-fault driver's insurance.

Question: Do I need a police report to file a claim?
Answer: A police report is not always required but is strongly recommended for accidents, theft, and vandalism. It provides official documentation that can help process your claim faster.

Question: What is subrogation?
Answer: Subrogation is when we seek reimbursement from the at-fault party's insurance company after paying your claim. If successful, you may get your deductible back.
