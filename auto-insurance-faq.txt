AUTO INSURANCE FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS - ABC INSURANCE

Question: What is a deductible?
Answer: A deductible is the amount you pay out of pocket before your insurance coverage kicks in. For example, if you have a $500 deductible and a $2000 claim, you pay $500 and we pay $1500.

Question: What is comprehensive coverage?
Answer: Comprehensive coverage protects your vehicle from non-collision damage like theft, vandalism, weather damage, fire, and hitting an animal. It's optional but recommended for newer vehicles.

Question: What is liability coverage?
Answer: Liability coverage pays for damage you cause to other people or their property in an accident. It includes bodily injury liability and property damage liability. This coverage is required by law in most states.

Question: What is collision coverage?
Answer: Collision coverage pays for damage to your car when you hit another vehicle or object, regardless of who is at fault. It covers repairs or replacement up to your car's actual cash value minus your deductible.

Question: What is the difference between collision and comprehensive?
Answer: Collision coverage pays for damage from hitting another vehicle or object. Comprehensive coverage pays for non-collision damage like theft, vandalism, weather, or fire. Both have separate deductibles.

Question: How do I add a vehicle to my policy?
Answer: To add a vehicle to your policy, call customer service with your vehicle information including year, make, model, and VIN number. You can also add vehicles through your online account.

Question: How do I remove a vehicle from my policy?
Answer: To remove a vehicle, call customer service at 1-800-555-0123 or log into your online account. You'll need to confirm the vehicle is no longer in your possession or provide proof of other coverage.

Question: What is uninsured motorist coverage?
Answer: Uninsured motorist coverage protects you if you're hit by a driver who doesn't have insurance or in a hit-and-run accident. It covers your medical bills and vehicle damage.

Question: What is personal injury protection?
Answer: Personal injury protection, or PIP, covers medical expenses for you and your passengers regardless of who caused the accident. It may also cover lost wages and other expenses.

Question: Does my insurance cover rental cars?
Answer: Your auto insurance typically extends to rental cars for personal use. Check your policy for rental car coverage details or call customer service to confirm your coverage.

Question: How can I lower my auto insurance premium?
Answer: You can lower your premium by increasing your deductible, bundling policies, maintaining a good driving record, taking defensive driving courses, or asking about available discounts.

Question: What discounts are available for auto insurance?
Answer: We offer discounts for safe drivers, multiple vehicles, bundling home and auto, good students, defensive driving courses, anti-theft devices, and paying in full.

Question: What happens if I get into an accident?
Answer: After an accident, ensure everyone is safe, call 911 if needed, exchange information with other drivers, take photos, and report the accident to us within 24 hours by calling 1-800-555-CLAIM.

Question: Will my rates go up if I file a claim?
Answer: Rate increases depend on the type and severity of the claim and your claims history. Not-at-fault accidents typically don't affect your rates. We can discuss specifics when you file.

Question: What is an SR-22?
Answer: An SR-22 is a certificate of financial responsibility that proves you have auto insurance. It's usually required after certain violations like DUI. We can help you file an SR-22 with your state.
