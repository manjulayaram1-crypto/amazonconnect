RENTERS INSURANCE FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS - ABC INSURANCE

Question: What is renters insurance?
Answer: Renters insurance protects your personal belongings in a rented apartment or home. It also provides liability coverage if someone is injured in your rental and covers additional living expenses if you're displaced.

Question: Does my landlord's insurance cover my stuff?
Answer: No, your landlord's insurance only covers the building structure, not your personal belongings. You need renters insurance to protect your furniture, electronics, clothing, and other possessions.

Question: How much does renters insurance cost?
Answer: Renters insurance is very affordable, typically between $15 and $30 per month depending on your coverage amount, location, and deductible. Bundling with auto insurance can save you more.

Question: What does renters insurance cover?
Answer: Renters insurance covers your personal property against theft, fire, vandalism, and certain water damage. It also includes liability protection and additional living expenses if your rental becomes uninhabitable.

Question: What is not covered by renters insurance?
Answer: Renters insurance typically does not cover floods, earthquakes, your roommate's belongings, damage from pests, or intentional damage. High-value items like jewelry may have coverage limits.

Question: How much renters insurance do I need?
Answer: Make a list of your belongings and estimate their total value. Most renters need $20,000 to $50,000 in personal property coverage. We recommend enough to replace all your possessions.

Question: Does renters insurance cover my laptop?
Answer: Yes, renters insurance covers your laptop, computer, and other electronics against theft, fire, and other covered events. Coverage applies at home and when you travel.

Question: Does renters insurance cover theft from my car?
Answer: Yes, if someone breaks into your car and steals your belongings, your renters insurance personal property coverage will pay for the stolen items minus your deductible.

Question: Am I covered if my apartment floods?
Answer: Standard renters insurance does not cover flood damage. If you live in a flood-prone area, you can purchase separate flood insurance. Water damage from burst pipes may be covered.

Question: Does renters insurance cover my roommate?
Answer: No, renters insurance only covers the named insured and their belongings. Your roommate needs their own renters insurance policy to protect their possessions.

Question: What is liability coverage in renters insurance?
Answer: Liability coverage protects you if someone is injured in your rental or if you accidentally damage someone's property. It pays for medical bills and legal expenses up to your policy limit.

Question: Does renters insurance cover bed bugs?
Answer: Typically, no. Pest infestations including bed bugs are generally not covered by renters insurance. Pest control is usually the responsibility of the landlord or tenant.

Question: Can I get renters insurance if I have a dog?
Answer: Yes, most renters insurance policies include liability coverage for dog bites. However, some breeds may be excluded. Contact us to confirm your dog is covered.

Question: Do I need renters insurance if I don't own much?
Answer: Yes, we recommend renters insurance even if you don't own expensive items. The liability coverage alone is valuable, and replacing all your belongings after a loss adds up quickly.

Question: How do I file a renters insurance claim?
Answer: To file a claim, call 1-800-555-CLAIM, use our mobile app, or file online. Document the damage with photos, make a list of lost or damaged items, and keep receipts if available.
